 The Problem

Civic Complaints Get Lost in the Gap

Jharkhand already runs portals like E-Jan Samadhan, but real problems on the ground still fall through the cracks before they reach someone who can actually solve them.

Scattered Reporting

Complaints come in through WhatsApp, CSC centres and web portals with no single unified system.

No Smart Routing

Problems aren't matched to the right university, department or industry partner who can fix them.

Duplicate & Unprioritised

Similar complaints pile up with no de-duplication or urgency ranking to guide action.

No Visibility

Citizens and government can't track resolution status or measure the real-world impact.

 The Solution

One Platform, Four Stakeholders

SICP brings citizens, academic institutions, industry and government onto a single collaborative platform.

Report Problems

Citizens report real, on-ground community challenges with location and evidence.

Solve & Innovate

Universities and students pick up challenges and build deployable prototypes.

Fund & Mentor

Industry and CSR partners support promising projects with funding and mentorship.

Monitor Impact

Government admins track outcomes and generate department-wide reports.

 Success Stories

Real Problems, Real Solutions

A few examples of complaints that were reported through SICP and successfully resolved.

 Gas leakage reported near JK Steels, Dhanbad
Handled byNIT JamshedpurResolved

The complaint was verified and passed on to the nearest engineering institute for inspection. The issue was fixed within 3 weeks and the site was cleared as safe by local authorities.

 Farmers in Ramgarh losing produce to dust & rain during open-air drying
Handled byLocal Agri-Tech InstituteResolved

A student team designed and built a low-cost solar-powered dryer. It is now being used by more than 40 farmers in the district, with industry support for wider rollout.

Built to Scale, Built to Last

Works with universities and colleges already active across Jharkhand, so problems reach people equipped to solve them.
